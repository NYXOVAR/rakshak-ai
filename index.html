Why checklists matter

A consistent checklist helps teams identify common hazards, verify basic preparedness and document follow-up actions. It supports—but does not replace—formal inspections and approved procedures.

A checked item means it was marked complete in this demo. The application cannot independently verify the physical condition of a site or asset.

Good inspection habits

  • Inspect carefully; never assume an item is safe.
  • Record defects and notify the responsible supervisor.
  • Do not attempt repairs beyond your training.
  • Recheck corrective actions and document closure.
